Whittle Studio Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 11, 2026

Whittle Studio is made by Dominic Feldi. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the Whittle Studio app on iPhone and iPad.

The short version

Whittle Studio does not collect, store, sell or share your personal data. There is no account or login, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no crash reporting. Your photos and edits stay on your device.

Your photos and edits

Everything you capture, import, edit, cut out or export is processed on your device. Photos you take, your editing history and your saved versions are stored in the app's own storage on your device. We have no servers that receive them and we never see them.

Permissions the app asks for

  • Camera: used only to show the live viewfinder and take photos when you choose to. Nothing from the camera is sent anywhere.
  • Add to Photos: used only to save images you export to your Photos library. The app asks for add-only access and cannot read your library through this permission.
  • Choosing photos: when you import a picture, you pick it with the system photo picker, so the app only receives the photos you select.

The app does not access your location, and photos taken in Whittle Studio do not contain location data.

Optional AI models

Some tools can use optional on-device AI models. They are never downloaded unless you tap Download. When you do, the app downloads the model files directly from Hugging Face (huggingface.co), a public model host. That request is an ordinary file download: like any web request, it reveals your device's IP address and basic request details to Hugging Face, but the app sends no photos, no account details and no identifiers of its own. Hugging Face's handling of that request is covered by its own privacy policy. Once downloaded, the models run entirely on your device and work offline. Downloaded models are excluded from iCloud backups, and you can delete them at any time in Settings > AI Models.

Settings

The app remembers your preferences, such as export format and tool settings, on your device only.

Sharing and exporting

Images only leave the app when you export or share them yourself, for example by saving to Photos, saving to Files or using the share sheet. What happens next is up to you and the service you choose.

Backups

Your device's own backup (iCloud or your computer) may include the app's data, as with any app. This is handled by Apple under your device settings, not by us.

Children

Whittle Studio does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Deleting your data

Because everything is stored on your device, you can delete it at any time by deleting items in the app or by deleting the app.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.
