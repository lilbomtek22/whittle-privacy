Whittle Studio Support

Current version: 1.0

Whittle Studio is a camera and image tool for iPhone, made by Dominic Feldi. This page answers the common questions. For anything else, write to me.

Contact

Email: supportwhittle@gmail.com

It helps if you tell me your iPhone model, your iOS version, the app version (Settings, then About), and what you were doing when something went wrong. Please do not send photos unless I ask for one.

Questions and answers

Where are my photos stored?

On your iPhone, in the app's own storage. Whittle Studio has no account and no servers that receive your pictures, so I never see them. Your phone's own backup (iCloud or your computer) may include the app's data, as with any app. Deleting the app deletes its photos, so save or share what you want to keep first. Read the full privacy policy for the details.

What are free exports, and what is the Full Version?

Whittle Studio is free to download. Taking photos, importing photos, saving to your Library and using every tool are free. Photos you take or import, camera filter copies and live cutouts export free, always. Pictures you have edited with the tools get 10 free exports. After that, exporting edited work needs the Full Version, a one-time purchase. There is no subscription and no account. Family Sharing is on, so people in your Family Sharing group can use the same purchase. Apple handles the payment; I receive no payment details.

How do I restore my purchase?

Open Settings from the Home screen, tap Full Version, then tap Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple Account you bought with, and be online. Restore works on a new phone, after you reinstall the app, and on a second device. If the button does nothing, check that you are signed in to the App Store on that phone, then try again. If it still does not work, email me.

How do I open a project file?

A project file ends in .whittle. It holds a project's pictures and edit settings, and you make one with Export Project. To open one, tap the file in the Files app and choose Whittle Studio, or open it from your Library with the Files option. It always opens as a new project, so it never replaces something you already have.

Do the AI models need the internet?

Some tools can use optional add-on models, such as Smart Select and AI Upscale. They download only when you tap Download in Settings, then AI Models, and they need a connection that one time. After that they run on your phone and work offline. Every tool still works without them. The models never make pictures for you, and no picture leaves your phone. You can delete a model at any time in the same place.

Our Story

After years of working in different software, just trying to bring my own little visions to life, I got worn down by the pricing and the endless "Sign up here!" screens. So I decided to make my own set of creative tools that does away with all of that.

My promise: any software made under my name is pay once, with no subscriptions, no ads, and no unnecessary accounts. These tools are meant to make your life easier and help you reach your creative vision as simply as possible, because that's what I always wanted for myself.

After dabbling with AI coding for game development, I had the idea during a long shift at work to point it somewhere else, and I invested in a Mac. Like many people, I was skeptical of AI and the slop the internet has rightly named. But I found that when it's used for the right purpose, and not as another get-rich-quick scheme, it lets one person build something that would normally take a bigger team and far more funding. That's the good use I want to put it to: making creative tools cheaper and more reachable, the way the future always promised.

Thank you for reading, and thank you for supporting Whittle Studio.
